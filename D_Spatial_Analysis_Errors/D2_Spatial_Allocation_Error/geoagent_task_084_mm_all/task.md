## 任务描述

Distribute the population of each Census block group to the individual building footprints within it, proportionally based on building areas. For instance, if a block group has a total population of 30 residents and contains two buildings with areas of 100 square meters and 200 square meters, assign 10 residents to the first building and 20 residents to the second, according to their respective area proportions. Save the results as a Shapefile containing at least the fields block_group_id, building_area and population.
